About OneVision Lawns & Gardens

An owner-operated lawn and garden business run by Lachlan Donohoe. One crew, two regions, and a simple promise: turn up when booked, do the whole job, leave the place clean.

The short version

One vision: lawns and gardens looked after properly

OneVision Lawns & Gardens was started by Lachlan Donohoe to do lawn and garden maintenance the way customers keep saying they cannot find: reliable, thorough and priced honestly.

We are not a franchise. There is no territory fee built into your price and no call centre between you and the person doing the work. When you call 0497 209 276, you get Lachlan. When the crew turns up, it is the same crew every visit, and they know your yard.

We work two regions: the Central Coast, from the Woy Woy Peninsula to Gwandalan, and Sydney's Upper North Shore and Hornsby Shire. The two are different jobs. Coastal lawns, sandy soil and holiday homes on one side; big established gardens, tall hedges and shaded lawns on the other. We are set up for both.

How we work

The standard on every job

Booked means booked

We keep a schedule. If rain moves it, you hear from us before the day, not after.

The whole job

Edges, whipper snipping, blow-down and clippings gone. A mowed lawn with the edges left is half a job.

Right for the site

Cut heights, pruning times, turf varieties and weed treatments chosen for your block, not copied from a template.

No surprises

Fixed price per visit. If the scope changes, you hear about the price before the work, not on the invoice.
